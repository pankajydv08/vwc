Petrichor: The smell of earth after rain

CharruuBangaram

క్షమించు (Kshaminchu)

Like the sun-parched earth of our Telugu heartlands,
I was burning too bright with anger, drying up the softness between us.
I said things I didn't mean, words that felt like heatwaves.

But you are the rain I've been waiting for.
Please forgive your Panku.
Let this be the moment the storm ends and the petrichor rises.

Only Yours, Panku
